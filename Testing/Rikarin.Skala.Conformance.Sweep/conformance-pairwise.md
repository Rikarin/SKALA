# Pairwise conformance sweep

Two options at once, over the whole grid of their values, Skala against
`jb cleanupcode`. Generated by `./build.sh Pairwise`; see docs/plan/12 §
"Interactions are out of scope", which named this pass and is what it answers.

⚠ **`INTERACTION` is the verdict this pass exists for.** It means every corner the
one-at-a-time sweep can reach agrees, and an interior corner does not — two options
that are each conformant alone and wrong together. `conformance-sweep.md` reports
both of them green and is not wrong to: it cannot reach the corner.

⚠ **`UNEXERCISED` is not a pass**, for the same reason it is not one there.

⚠ **`INHERITED` is not a finding about the pair either.** Every corner that
disagrees does so at a value where `conformance-sweep.json` already records one of
the two keys diverging on its own. 17 rows of this pass's first corrected run were
this, all of them `max_line_length` at `0` and `1`.

⚠ **`BASELINE` is not a finding about the pair.** The two engines already disagreed
on that fixture before either key was set, so its grid answers nothing — the work is
in `docs/divergences.md`, not here.
